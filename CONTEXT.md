# convoapp

A tool for practising conversational fluency. It shows and speaks a prompt; you
answer it out loud, in real life, and the app times how long you took. The app
never hears you — it paces the practice, it does not judge it.

## Language

**Prompt**:
A single line that someone says to you, phrased so the natural reaction is to
answer it rather than read it.
_Avoid_: question, phrase, item

**Scene**:
Stage direction naming who is speaking to you on a given card, such as
*Landscaping company*. Shown on screen, never spoken aloud.
_Avoid_: category, context, setting, situation

**Card**:
One prompt in play: shown, spoken, and timed until you dismiss it.
_Avoid_: flashcard, slide, question

**Deck**:
One named collection of prompts, such as *Small Talk* or *Business Calls*.
_Avoid_: mode, category, topic, pack

**Mixed**:
The draw that pools every deck together, so a run is not filtered to one
subject.
_Avoid_: all, both, random, shuffle

**Run**:
A sequence of cards, ended either by reaching the card count or by quitting.
_Avoid_: session, game, round, quiz

**Summary**:
The screen shown at the end of a run, pairing every prompt with how long you
took to answer it.
_Avoid_: results, score, report, stats

## Roles

**Caller**:
You, on a Business Calls card. The app speaks as the business; you are the
customer who has rung in.
_Avoid_: user, customer, client
